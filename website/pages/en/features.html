Features

What Rengaine does, from your files to your ears.

A library that files itself

Rengaine reads a folder or an S3 bucket and never modifies your files. One folder is one album; title, artist and year are voted from the tracks' tags. An MP3 and a FLAC of the same song are one track, and the lossless one is played.

Drop a new album in the inbox/ folder: it is filed under a clean name (Artist/Album (year)/01 - Title.flac). Covers, genres, years: every correction is kept, survives rescans and can be undone.

{{shot:album|An album page: cover, details, track list with durations}}

Careful sound

Every track is measured: volume is normalised (per track or per album), leading and trailing silences are skipped. When the tempo is known, the cross-fade lasts whole bars and lines up the beats, like a DJ.

On a phone or a slow connection, a lighter Opus version is converted on the fly, with one setting for wifi and one for mobile data.

{{shot:settings|Sound settings: volume normalisation, cross-fade and stream quality}}

Endless listening

Explore by genre and sub-genre, by mood (calm, focus, energetic, dance), by decade or by collection, and start a shuffle that never stops.

Continuous play picks tracks that follow the last one well: key, tempo, favourites, no recent repeats, artists spaced out.

{{shot:explore|The Explore page: genres, moods and decades}}

For the whole household

Every account has its own queue, favourites, playlists, history and settings. The queue follows you from one device to another: started on the computer, it continues on the phone.

Account requests are approved by an admin; everyone sees their connected devices and can sign them out.

{{shot:queue|The queue open next to the player, with the current track}}

A remote control to share

At a party, send your guests a link. From their phone, without an account, they add songs, reorder, pause or turn the volume up, depending on the rights you chose. The link expires on its own, and you can revoke it at any time.

{{shot:remote-phone|A guest's remote control on a phone: the current track and what comes next}}

AI proposes, you decide

A large library has messy genres, missing years, artists spelled two ways. Rengaine has an MCP server: any compatible client can read the library and propose corrections, grouped in batches.

Nothing changes until you apply a batch, and every batch can be reverted. With an API key, or a local model, the request can also start from the admin.

{{shot:proposals|The proposals screen: a batch of changes proposed by an AI, to apply or reject}}

Yours, and private

No tracker, no font or script loaded from elsewhere, no external call you did not configure. IP addresses are erased after a configurable delay. Everyone can download their data, delete their listening history and delete their account.
