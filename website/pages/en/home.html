Your music library, self-hosted.

Rengaine is a music server and player for the albums you own. Point it at a folder of MP3 and FLAC files, then listen from any browser or from the Android app, at home or on the go.

Install Rengaine See the features

  • Your files, untouched

    One folder is one album; tags are read, never rewritten. Drop new albums in an inbox folder and they are filed under clean names.

  • Sound that flows

    Loudness normalisation, silences skipped, cross-fades that line up the beats, and lighter Opus streams for phones.

  • Endless listening

    Shuffle by genre, mood, decade or collection. Continuous play picks tracks that follow well.

  • For the whole household

    Accounts with their own queue, favourites, playlists and history, synchronised between the browser and the Android app.

  • Shared remote control

    Send a guest a link: from their phone, they add tracks and control the music, without an account.

  • AI-assisted, never AI-driven

    Any MCP client can read your library and propose corrections. You apply them, or not; every change can be undone.

  • Yours, and private

    No tracker, no external call you did not configure, IP addresses erased after a delay, data export and account deletion for everyone.

  • Simple to run

    PHP, MariaDB and FFmpeg on any Linux server. No Composer, no container required, a folder or an S3 bucket for storage.

Rengaine is free software under the MIT license. Its interface is available in English and French. The albums in the screenshots are fictional: artists, covers and music were made up for the demonstration.
