The Android app

Rengaine for Android puts your library in your pocket. The app connects to your own Rengaine server and plays your music in the background, on the lock screen or over Bluetooth. It provides no music and sends nothing anywhere but your server.

Download the APK from GitHub

Pick up where you left off

Your queue, favourites, playlists and settings are the same as on the website: listening started on the computer continues on the phone. To link the app, scan the QR code shown by the website (Settings › Connected devices › Pair a device), or enter the server address, your email and your password. Several accounts can live side by side.

What it does

  • Albums, Artists, Explore (moods, genres, decades), Shuffle, Playlists, My favourites;
  • search as you type, listening history;
  • queue synchronised with the website, endless mode, continuous play;
  • waveform in the player;
  • playlists: create, rename, share, add and remove tracks;
  • connected devices (and pairing another device), data export, account deletion;
  • light or dark theme, in English and French.

Made for the phone

  • background playback, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen;
  • headset and Bluetooth buttons;
  • a cache of tracks already played (250 MB to 5 GB), which start again instantly;
  • stream quality based on the network: a lighter Opus version on mobile data;
  • plays kept on the phone while offline, then sent when the network is back.

Planned: changing your name and password in the app (on the website for now), reordering the tracks of a playlist, cross-fades, background images, tablets, shared remote control, downloads for offline listening, Android Auto, widget, Chromecast.

Download

Test version 1.0.0-rc.1, for Android 12 or later. The APK (rengaine-1.0.0-rc.1.apk) is in the release on GitHub. A Play Store release is planned.

  • No automatic updates outside the Play Store: watch the releases, or use Obtainium.
  • A server address in https:// is recommended; http:// is accepted, with a warning.
