The Android app

Rengaine for Android puts your library in your pocket. The app connects to your own Rengaine server and plays your music in the background, on the lock screen or over Bluetooth. It provides no music and sends nothing anywhere but your server.

Pick up where you left off

Your queue, favourites, playlists and settings are the same as on the website: listening started on the computer continues on the phone. To link the app, scan the QR code shown by the website (Settings › Connected devices › Pair a device), or type the server address: no password to type. Several accounts can live side by side.

What it does

  • Albums, Artists, Explore (moods, genres, decades), Shuffle, Playlists, My favourites;
  • search as you type, listening history;
  • queue synchronised with the website, endless mode;
  • light or dark theme, in English and French.

Made for the phone

  • background playback, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen;
  • headset and Bluetooth buttons;
  • a cache of tracks already played (250 MB to 5 GB), which start again instantly;
  • stream quality based on the network: a lighter Opus version on mobile data;
  • plays kept on the phone while offline, then sent when the network is back.

Coming soon: account and device management, continuous play, waveform in the player, full playlist management.
