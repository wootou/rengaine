Download

Rengaine runs on a Linux server of your own: a mini PC at home, a NAS, a rented server.

What you need

  • a recent Debian or Ubuntu, PHP 8 with PHP-FPM, MariaDB, Apache (or Nginx) and FFmpeg;
  • your music in a folder, or in S3-compatible storage;
  • optional: Essentia (tempo and key), Chromaprint (duplicate recordings), Mercure (shared remote control), an SMTP relay (mail).
